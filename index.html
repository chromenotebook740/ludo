Ludo Rules

• Start: Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of the base yard to your start tile.
• Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or reaching home grants an immediate Bonus Roll.
• 3 Sixes Rule: Rolling 6 three consecutive times forfeits the roll and passes the turn.
• Captures: Landing on an opponent’s pawn sends it back to base and awards a bonus turn.
• 8 Safe Zones: 4 colored starting squares + 4 Star squares (★) are safe zones where no captures can occur.
• 2-Player Mode: Players are positioned diagonally opposite (Red vs Yellow) for balanced play.
